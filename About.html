Welcome to Our World

Every Celebration

Welcome to Event Planner by Iqra, where creativity, elegance and thoughtful details come together to make your special occasions memorable.

We believe every celebration is unique. Whether it's a graceful Nikaah ceremony, a colourful Mehndi celebration, a birthday surprise or a beautifully decorated room, every occasion deserves a personal touch.

Our focus is on transforming your ideas into beautiful event settings through carefully chosen colours, floral arrangements, lighting and decorative details.
