How can I book Event Planner by Iqra?

Complete the booking inquiry form above or contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email. Share your event date, location and service requirements.

Yes. Our listed service areas include Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Bhara Kahu. Contact us to discuss your exact venue location.

You can share your preferred colours, theme and inspiration photographs to discuss a personalised decoration concept.
