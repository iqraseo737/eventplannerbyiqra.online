Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book Event Planner by Iqra?

Fill the inquiry form, or contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email. Share your event date, venue and the services you need.

Do you provide catering, Qawwali and sound as well as decoration?

Yes. Besides stage and venue decoration, we arrange catering and food, Qawwali performers, sound systems and DJ, photography and videography, all under one plan.

Do you serve Islamabad and Rawalpindi?

Yes. We serve Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Bhara Kahu. Contact us with your exact venue to confirm.

Can I choose my own theme and colours?

Absolutely. Share your colours, inspiration photos and budget and we will design a personalised concept.

How much does an event cost?

Cost depends on the event type, venue, guest count, menu and decoration. Send us your details and we will provide a clear quotation.

How early should I contact you?

As early as possible, especially during the wedding season, so we can confirm your date and plan every detail.
