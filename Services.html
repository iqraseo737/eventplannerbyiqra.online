Frequently Asked Questions

Which areas do you serve?

Event Planner by Iqra serves Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Bhara Kahu. Contact us to discuss your exact event location.

Can I request a custom decoration theme?

Yes, you can share your preferred colours, inspiration pictures, event type and decoration requirements to discuss a personalised setup.

How can I book an event?

You can contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp. Share your event date, location, service requirements and preferred style.

Photography and videography are included in our listed services. Please contact us to discuss coverage options and availability.

How much does event decoration cost?

Pricing depends on the event type, decoration requirements, venue, materials and design complexity. Contact us for a quotation.
